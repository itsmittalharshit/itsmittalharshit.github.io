I'm a Master's student in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Leeds, graduating with a First Class in September 2026. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, medical image analysis, and practical software engineering.

My thesis focuses on SAPNeXt — a novel hierarchical encoder-decoder for nuclei instance segmentation in histopathology images, achieving state-of-the-art results across 19 tissue types on the PanNuke pan-cancer benchmark.

Beyond research, I build offline-first mobile apps with Flutter — tools designed to work reliably without internet connectivity. I've interned as an AI researcher at USIC&T and as a project trainee at CFEES, DRDO, contributing to peer-reviewed work in Springer and IEEE frameworks.

Research

Academic Work

📄 MSc Thesis · 2026
The Design and Implementation of SAPNeXt: A Novel Hierarchical Encoder-Decoder for Nuclei Instance Segmentation

SAPNeXt integrates Spatial Adaptive Fusion and PixelShuffle upsampling over a pretrained ConvNeXt backbone into a hierarchical encoder-decoder pipeline. Trained and evaluated on the MoNuSeg test set and PanNuke pan-cancer benchmark, it outperforms CellViT, HoVerNeXt, and SwinUNet across 19 tissue types while cutting inference time to just 1.6 seconds per whole-slide image.
